Our Approach

Tyber.io is not a generic AI consultancy. We are production engineers who know where AI pays off — and where it does not. We focus on the boring-but-critical layer where AI meets uptime: code review automation, intelligent monitoring, operations bots, RAG over corporate knowledge, and delivery pipelines augmented by intelligence. Every recommendation we make is rooted in systems we have actually run, not hypotheticals from a slide deck. We work hands-on, ship in iterations, and document decisions so the work can survive after we leave.
